    RESEED

    Released Apr 2026 · 2-person team · Steam

    A puzzle platformer played in two phases. First, plan your route through an empty, safe level. Then a little seed follows your exact moves through the same level, now full of traps. If it dies, adjust your route and try again.

    What I did

    • Programmed the entire game and shipped it on Steam in 3 months, from first prototype to release.
    • Built the record-and-replay core loop that captures the player's planned path and replays it through the trap-filled level.
    • Covered the full release scope: control remapping with Unity's new Input System, localization into 8 languages, colorblind options and Steam achievements.
